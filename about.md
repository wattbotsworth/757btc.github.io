757btc is a community of Bitcoiners, builders, and freedom tech enthusiasts in the Hampton Roads, Virginia area.

We meet on the **second Saturday of every month** at [Mile Zero Marine](https://milezeromarine.com/), 115 High Street, Portsmouth, VA 23704.

Our meetups are **free and family-friendly**. We cover Bitcoin, Lightning, Nostr, privacy tools, and whatever else the community wants to explore.

### What We Believe

- **Bitcoin is money.** Sound, permissionless, uncensorable.
- **Open source is freedom.** We build with and contribute to open source tools.
- **Privacy is a right.** We default to privacy-respecting technology.
- **Community matters.** Local connections are the foundation of resilience.

### History

757btc has been meeting monthly since February 2024. We've hosted over 25 consecutive meetups, organized sats markets with Lightning-powered beer taps, and continue to grow the Bitcoin community in Hampton Roads.
